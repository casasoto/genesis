# LocksmithPrep — Product Summary

## What It Is
LocksmithPrep is a cross-platform, offline-capable Progressive Web App that helps users prepare for ALOA (Associated Locksmiths of America) beginner-level locksmith certification exams through adaptive learning, multimedia lessons, practice exams, and AI-assisted tutoring.

## Who It's For
- Aspiring locksmiths preparing for ALOA beginner certification
- Locksmith apprentices seeking structured study material
- Trade school students supplementing classroom learning

## Core Value Proposition
- **Structured preparation** — auto-generated study plans based on user-set timeframes
- **Adaptive learning** — difficulty adjusts based on performance
- **Offline access** — full functionality without internet (PWA + IndexedDB)
- **AI tutoring** — expert-level explanations grounded in verified content with citations
- **Spaced repetition** — scientifically-backed flashcard scheduling (SM-2 algorithm)
- **Human-verified content** — editorial workflow ensures accuracy and provenance

## Key Differentiators
1. **Content provenance** — every fact is traceable to source material with citations
2. **Human-in-the-loop AI** — low-confidence AI responses are flagged for expert review
3. **Ethical guardrails** — AI tutor refuses to provide illegal lock bypass instructions
4. **Extensible architecture** — designed to scale from beginner to advanced certifications without breaking existing functionality

## Disclaimers
- Does NOT guarantee passing any exam
- Not affiliated with ALOA
- Users should supplement with supervised hands-on practice
- Content accuracy relies on editorial review process
